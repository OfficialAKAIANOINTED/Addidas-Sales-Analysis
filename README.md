# Adidas US Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard)

**Business question:** Where does Adidas make its US sales and profit (by retailer, region, product and sales channel), and how did sales change from 2020 to 2021?

**Data:** Adidas US sales database with 9,648 transactions from 1 Jan 2020 to 31 Dec 2021. Columns: retailer, invoice date, region, state, city, product, price per unit, units sold, total sales, operating profit, operating margin and sales method.

## Method

1. **Analysis (Excel PivotTables):** total sales and operating profit by retailer, region, state, product, sales method and month.
2. **Dashboard** (`Adidas_Dashboard.xlsx`): Excel dashboard summarising sales by retailer, region, state, sales method and month.

## Key findings

- **Total:** about $900M in sales and $332M in operating profit (about 37% margin).
- **Growth:** sales rose from about $182M in 2020 to about $718M in 2021.
- **Top product:** Men's Street Footwear ($209M sales, about 40% margin), followed by Women's Apparel ($179M).
- **Retailers:** West Gear ($243M) and Foot Locker ($220M) sold the most; Sports Direct had the best margin (about 41%).
- **Regions:** the West led sales ($270M) but had the lowest margin (about 33%); the South had the highest margin (about 42%).
- **Top states:** New York ($64M), California ($60M) and Florida ($59M).
- **Channels:** in-store brought the most sales ($357M), but online had the best margin (about 39%).

## Recommendations

- Push Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel harder, especially online where margins are highest.
- Review pricing and costs in the West to lift its margin.
- Grow the Sports Direct partnership, which converts sales into profit most efficiently.

## Tools

Microsoft Excel (PivotTables and charts).
